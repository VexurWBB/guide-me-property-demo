The difference

What makes Guide Me Property different

  • Guide Me Property is built around Alfred's own experience as an investor, not around a sales target. Recommendations are made with your best interests at heart, with honest advice and strategic guidance at every step.
  • The work spans residential and commercial property, strategy before search, and support that continues after settlement. You are not passed between departments or left to interpret jargon on your own.
  • At 48, Alfred chooses to keep working because the mission matters: to educate, guide, and support people across Australia as they build lasting wealth through property.

The relationship

How the relationship works

  • Every engagement starts with understanding where you are, what you want to achieve, and what you need explained in plain language. Strategy is agreed before any property search begins.
  • Throughout the purchase path, you stay involved in decisions while Guide Me Property handles research, diligence, negotiation, and coordination with trusted professionals when required.
  • After settlement, the relationship continues through portfolio review and planning for what comes next, so each purchase connects to a longer direction rather than standing alone.
