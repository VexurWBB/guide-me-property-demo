What happens

We start with an open conversation, not a sales pitch. You share where you are in life, what you already own, how property fits your plans, and what you want it to achieve over time. We ask about your timeline, your comfort with risk, and any constraints that should shape the work ahead. This is the moment to ask questions, voice concerns, and get plain language answers before anything is committed.

Your role

Come ready to talk honestly about your situation and goals. You do not need to arrive with a suburb list or a property type already chosen. Share what you know, what you are unsure about, and what would make this process feel manageable for you.

Guide Me Property's role

We listen carefully, explain how buyer's agency works in practice, and outline what a structured engagement could look like for your circumstances. We clarify what we can help with, what sits outside our role, and whether Guide Me Property is the right fit before moving forward.

Outcome

You leave with a clear picture of the path ahead and whether we should work together. If we proceed, you have a shared understanding of your starting point and what success looks like for you.

What happens

Before any listing is opened, we define the plan your purchase should serve. That includes ownership structure, asset type, location criteria, and the sequence of decisions that matter for your goals. We agree on what a suitable property looks like, what to avoid, and how this purchase connects to what comes next. The search brief is written down so every later decision can be tested against it.

Your role

Engage with the strategy conversation and challenge assumptions where they do not feel right. Confirm priorities, flag personal constraints, and sign off on a brief you understand and trust before the search begins.

Guide Me Property's role

We translate your goals into a practical investment strategy and a clear property brief. We explain the reasoning behind structure, market selection, and asset type so you can make informed choices rather than reactive ones.

Outcome

A documented strategy and search brief that guides every step that follows. You know what you are looking for, why it matters, and how this purchase fits the bigger picture.

What happens

We analyse markets and suburbs against your brief, separating compelling stories from properties that actually fit your plan. Sourcing covers on market listings and off market opportunities that may never reach a portal. Promising options are shortlisted, inspected, and tested through structured due diligence before you are asked to commit. You receive clear summaries of findings, risks, and trade-offs for each contender.

Your role

Review shortlists and due diligence reports, attend inspections when useful, and give timely feedback on what aligns with your brief. Raise questions early so nothing important is left unresolved.

Guide Me Property's role

We conduct suburb and market analysis, source properties on and off market, coordinate inspections, and complete due diligence on every shortlisted asset. We present findings in plain language and recommend whether to proceed, renegotiate, or walk away.

Outcome

A thoroughly researched shortlist and a recommended property backed by evidence, not enthusiasm. You understand the asset, the market context, and the risks before purchase.

What happens

Once a property is selected, we handle negotiation so you are not bidding against your own emotions. We work toward terms that reflect the due diligence findings and your strategy, then manage the purchase through contract, conditions, and settlement. Brokers, solicitors, accountants, and other professionals are introduced when their expertise adds value, not before.

Your role

Stay reachable for decisions that require your approval, review contracts and advice from your solicitor, and complete finance and identity steps on time. Trust the process while remaining informed at each milestone.

Guide Me Property's role

We negotiate price and terms, liaise with selling agents, coordinate your professional team, and keep the purchase on track through to settlement. We advocate for your interests and communicate progress clearly throughout.

Outcome

A secured property on terms aligned with your strategy, with the purchase managed through to settlement. You have professional support at every stage without carrying the negotiation burden alone.

What happens

After settlement, the work does not stop. We revisit your holdings on a regular cycle to check performance against your original plan, identify what has changed in your life or the market, and prepare for what comes next. Reviews cover whether each property still serves its role, whether structure or financing should be adjusted, and how the next purchase should be sequenced.

Your role

Participate in scheduled reviews, share updates on your circumstances, and stay open to adjusting the plan when life or markets shift. Treat portfolio management as an ongoing discipline, not a one-off event.

Guide Me Property's role

We conduct regular portfolio reviews, flag issues early, and help you plan the next move with the same rigour applied to your first purchase. We remain available as a long term partner, not a transaction-only service.

Outcome

A portfolio that stays aligned with your goals over time. The next purchase is planned deliberately, supported by review and strategy rather than improvised under pressure.
